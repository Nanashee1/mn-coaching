Core Curriculum

A process-oriented framework designed to develop consistency, composure, and total table control.

Module 1

Mechanics & Alignment

Establish a repeatable, smooth stroke, a solid stance, and precise vision center alignment.

Module 2

Position & Cue Ball Control

Master the 90° tangent line and 30° rolling ball line, natural shape routes, and self-diagnosis techniques.

Module 3

Technical Shot-Making

Develop precision visualization techniques, speed control, kicking, and banking.

Module 4

Mental Game & Composure

Build unbreakable composure under match pressure using breathing techniques, structured pre-shot processes, and personal accountability.

Module 5

Break Strategy & Table Mapping

Optimize break accuracy, read rack dynamics, and map multi-ball run-out routes efficiently.
