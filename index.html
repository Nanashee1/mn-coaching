Player & Coach Perspective

My passion for the game comes from a relentless obsession with execution and stroke mechanics. As a competitive pool player, my proudest tournament highlight was placing T-5th out of over 1,000 players at the Super Billiards Expo Open Amateur Players Championship.

I focus on teaching what actually holds up under competitive pressure: solid stance alignment, repeatable stroke dynamics, and a structured, analytical routine to fix errors live at the table.

Coaching Philosophy

  • ✓ Fundamentals Build Consistency: A repeatable game requires an unflappable foundation.
  • ✓ Process Over Outcome: Respecting your routine and execution breeds long-term victory.
  • ✓ Self-Diagnosis: Learning to diagnose why an error occurred so you can correct it instantly.

The Coaching Progression

A structured 5-pillar pathway from mechanical basics to competitive mastery.

Pillar 01

Mechanics & Alignment

Establish a repeatable, wobble-free stroke, solid stance geometry, and precise vision center alignment.

Pillar 02

Position & Physics

Master 90° and 30° tangent lines, natural shape routes, and self-diagnostic error correction.

Pillar 03

Technical Shot-Making

Learn high-stress execution including spin-induced throw, kicking, banking, and fractional aiming.

Pillar 04

Mental Game & Composure

Build unbreakable composure under match pressure using box breathing, reset routines, and accountability.

Pillar 05

Break Strategy & Table Management

Optimize energy transfer on the break, read rack spreads, and map multi-ball runout routes efficiently.
